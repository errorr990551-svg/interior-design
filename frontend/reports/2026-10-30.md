### QA results
- First run: 3 failures, all fixed. Two Pune figures had no source marker, and the Nashik meta description was 159 characters.
- Final run: all automated gates (1–6) pass for both pages. The highest overlap between them is 2.0%.
- Gate 7 (manual self-review), done:
  - The two pages lead with different angles. Pune focuses on its housing corridors and rental flats. Nashik focuses on ready-to-move buyers, second homes and owners managing work from Mumbai or Pune.
  - The climate guidance differs too: Pune's is about the four-month monsoon, Nashik's about the swing from very dry springs to a heavy July.
- Rendering checked at 390 px and 1280 px: no horizontal scroll, tables fit, and Livspace links carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.

### Claims removed because they could not be verified
- **Livspace's Pune page:**
  - It gives two different "homes delivered" counts (18,073+ and 16,601+), so neither is used.
  - It describes the warranty as "lifetime" in one place and "10-year" in another. Both pages tell readers to get the warranty in writing.
- **Livspace FAQ price ranges** (₹/sq ft, package bands): these are marketing estimates with no stated basis. On the Nashik page they contradict its own flat sizes. Only the dated "starting at" prices are quoted.
- **Other items left out:**
  - typical carpet areas (no credible current source)
  - customer reviews and ratings
  - experience-centre phone numbers and addresses
  - Livspace's ₹50,000 consultation-only claim
  - Nashik growth-corridor map labels (couldn't be read reliably)
- Full list with reasons: `removed_claims` in each `data/facts/{city}.json`.
