## Who lives in Ulhasnagar's homes

Ulhasnagar Municipal Corporation had 506,098 residents in the 2011 Census{{f:ulhasnagar-census-pop}}, in 110,234 households{{f:ulhasnagar-hh-total}}. The Census counts living rooms, bedrooms, dining and study rooms as dwelling rooms, and excludes kitchens, bathrooms, store rooms and verandahs{{f:census-room-definition}}.

Only 22.1% of households rented{{f:ulhasnagar-hh-rented}}, one of the lowest shares among the region's corporations, while 76.5% owned their home{{f:ulhasnagar-hh-owned}}. Homes were compact, with most households having two rooms or fewer.

When people own a home for a long time, the interior work changes. Rather than furnishing an empty new flat, the job is usually to refresh rooms that have been lived in for years: replacing a worn kitchen, adding storage, updating wiring and fixing damp. This guide focuses on that kind of project.

## Start with what you cannot see

In a home that has been lived in for many years, the most important work is often hidden. Deal with it before you choose laminates and colours.

**Electrical.** Old wiring and distribution boards were not designed for several ACs, a microwave, a washing machine and a geyser running together. Ask an electrician to check the load, the condition of the wires and the earthing. Rewiring before new wardrobes and false ceilings go in is much cheaper than afterwards.

**Plumbing.** Concealed pipes in kitchens and bathrooms may be corroded or leaking. If you are replacing the kitchen or retiling a bathroom, it is the right moment to replace old pipes too.

**Damp and seepage.** Look for flaking paint, salt deposits and dark patches, especially on external walls and on the ceiling below the bathroom above. Waterproofing comes before anything else.

**Floors and walls.** Hollow tiles, cracks and uneven surfaces should be repaired or levelled before furniture is fitted against them.

**Ask for a site inspection before the quote.** A designer who quotes without seeing these things will either add them later or ignore them.

## Renovating while you live at home

Most owner-occupiers in Ulhasnagar will stay at home during the work. A little planning makes that bearable.

**Work room by room.** Agree a sequence, for example bedroom first, then kitchen, then living room, so that at least one bedroom and the bathroom are always usable.

**Plan a temporary kitchen.** While the kitchen is being replaced, set up an induction hob, a kettle and a small table in another room. Kitchen work usually takes longest, so schedule it when the family can manage.

**Contain the dust.** Ask for plastic sheeting over doorways, covered furniture and daily clean-up. Cutting and sanding can be done on a balcony or outside the flat where the society allows.

**Agree working hours.** Societies usually set them; families with young children or elderly members may want quieter hours on top of that.

**Store valuables and documents safely** before work starts, and keep a list of what was moved where.

## Refreshing compact rooms

With most homes at two rooms or fewer{{f:ulhasnagar-hh-small,ulhasnagar-hh-two}}, renovation is a chance to fit in storage that the original layout lacked.

- **Replace freestanding cupboards with full-height wardrobes.** One built-in unit along the longest wall usually holds more than two old steel cupboards.
- **Add lofts above doors and passages** for suitcases and seasonal items.
- **Upgrade the bed.** A hydraulic storage bed adds a full wardrobe's worth of space.
- **Rethink the kitchen.** Tall units, corner solutions and a dedicated slot for the water purifier and gas cylinder make a small kitchen far more usable.
- **Simplify the living room.** A wall-mounted TV unit, a sofa-cum-bed for guests and a fold-down table keep the floor clear.
- **Lighten the palette.** Light walls, light laminates and one feature wall make compact rooms feel larger.

## Materials for the monsoon months

Ulhasnagar has no IMD observatory with full 1991–2020 normals of its own{{f:ulhasnagar-imd-rain}}. The nearest full record, at Santacruz, shows about 96% of annual rain falling from June to September{{f:ulhasnagar-imd-monsoon}}, with morning humidity of 86% in July and August{{f:ulhasnagar-imd-humidity}}.

- **Boards:** boiling-water-resistant or boiling-water-proof plywood for kitchens, bathrooms and outer-wall wardrobes.
- **Edges:** sealed on every side.
- **Shutters:** laminate or acrylic for easy cleaning and humidity resistance.
- **Hardware:** stainless or well-coated hinges and channels from a known brand.
- **Timing:** schedule waterproofing before June, and paint or polish in the drier months.

In a renovation, there is one more consideration: old furniture you plan to keep. Check the backs and bases of existing cupboards for swelling, termite tracks and mould before you move them back against freshly painted walls. Treat or replace anything affected, so that a new finish is not spoiled by an old problem.

## Comparing renovation quotes

Ulhasnagar is in Thane district, which the state reports together with Palghar; the combined district had a per capita income of ₹3,90,726 in 2023-24{{f:ulhasnagar-district-income}}. Renovation quotes are harder to compare than new fit-outs, because so much depends on what is found on site.

**Separate repairs from furniture.** Electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, tiling and painting should each be their own line, with a clear scope.

**Ask how surprises are handled.** If rotten pipes or bad wiring are discovered, how will the extra work be priced and approved? A simple rule works: nothing extra without a written note of the cost and the effect on the schedule.

**Name the materials.** Board grade and brand, shutter finish, hardware brand and countertop on every furniture line.

**Pay against progress.** Link payments to completed rooms or stages, and keep a final share until the snag list is closed.

**Agree protection and clean-up** in writing, especially if you are staying at home.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I rewire my flat during a renovation?

If the wiring is old or you are adding ACs and kitchen appliances, yes. Get an electrician to check the load and earthing first; rewiring before new furniture goes in is far cheaper.

### Can we stay at home while the kitchen is redone?

Usually yes, with a temporary kitchen in another room. Plan the kitchen work for a period when the family can manage without it.

### How do I add storage to an older flat?

Replace freestanding cupboards with full-height wardrobes, add lofts, use a storage bed and rethink the kitchen with tall units. In 2011, most Ulhasnagar households had two rooms or fewer{{f:ulhasnagar-hh-small,ulhasnagar-hh-two}}.

### When should waterproofing be done?

Before the monsoon and before any furniture is fixed to the affected walls. At the nearest IMD observatory, about 96% of the year's rain falls between June and September{{f:ulhasnagar-imd-monsoon}}.

### How do I compare renovation quotes fairly?

Ask for repairs and furniture to be listed separately, with materials named, and agree how unexpected work will be priced before you sign.
