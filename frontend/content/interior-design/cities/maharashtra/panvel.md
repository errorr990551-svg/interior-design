## What Panvel covers now

Panvel was a municipal council from 1852 and became a municipal corporation in 2016{{f:panvel-pmc}}. The corporation includes 29 revenue villages of Panvel taluka and the planned CIDCO colonies of Taloja, Kharghar, Kalamboli, Kamothe and New Panvel{{f:panvel-pmc}}. When people say they are buying "in Panvel", they may mean any of these areas.

That matters for reading the Census figures below. The 2011 Census counted 180,020 residents in the old Panvel municipal council{{f:panvel-census-pop}}, in 42,466 households{{f:panvel-hh-total}}. That is the older town only, not the CIDCO colonies that later joined the corporation. It still gives a useful picture of the older housing.

| Dwelling rooms per household (old council area) | Share of households |
|---|---|
| One room, or no exclusive room{{f:panvel-hh-small}} | 22.1% |
| Two rooms{{f:panvel-hh-two}} | 32.9% |
| Three or more rooms{{f:panvel-hh-large}} | 44.9% |

The Census counts living rooms, bedrooms, dining and study rooms, not kitchens or bathrooms{{f:census-room-definition}}, so three rooms is roughly a 2BHK. By that measure, homes in the old town were larger than in most of the Mumbai region. Seven in ten households owned their home{{f:panvel-hh-owned}}, and 27.3% rented{{f:panvel-hh-rented}}.

## Fitting out a new flat in Kharghar, Kamothe or Taloja

Much of the new housing in the Panvel corporation area is in recently built towers. A new flat is easier to work in than an old one, but the fit-out still needs planning.

**Start at possession.** Walk through with your designer. Test every electrical point and switch, look for seepage around windows and in bathrooms, and check the floor tiles for hollow spots. Get the builder to fix defects before handover is complete.

**Ask for the drawings.** The builder's electrical and plumbing layouts show where concealed pipes and wires run. Your designer needs them before drilling into walls for wardrobes and TV units.

**Decide what stays.** A new flat comes with a kitchen platform, sink, tiles and bathroom fittings. Keeping what is sound frees budget for storage and lighting.

**Plan the electrical layout around the furniture.** Builders place points for a generic layout. If your bed, TV, study desk or kitchen appliances go elsewhere, move the points now, before walls are painted and wardrobes are built.

**Check the society rules for interior work.** New societies often have strict rules on working hours, lift use, debris and deposits, and some require a no-objection letter before work begins.

## Living near the airport

Navi Mumbai International Airport began commercial operations on 25 December 2025, initially operating from 8 am to 8 pm{{f:panvel-airport}}. For homeowners in nearby parts of Panvel, two things are worth thinking about.

**Noise.** Not every area will be affected, and flight paths matter more than distance. If your flat is under a flight path, consider windows with better acoustic glazing and seals, heavier curtains, upholstered headboards, rugs and bookshelves on shared walls. Ask any window supplier for the acoustic rating of both glass and frame.

**Rental demand.** An airport brings jobs and visitors, and some owners may decide to let their flat. If that is your plan, keep fixed joinery to the kitchen and wardrobes and choose durable, neutral, easy-to-clean finishes.

## Panvel's monsoon and your materials

Panvel has no IMD observatory with full 1991–2020 normals of its own{{f:panvel-imd-rain}}. The nearest full record, at Santacruz in Mumbai, shows about 96% of the year's rain falling from June to September, with July the wettest month{{f:panvel-imd-monsoon}}. Morning humidity there averages 86% in July and August{{f:panvel-imd-humidity}}.

That calls for:

- **Moisture-resistant boards** in kitchens, bathrooms and wardrobes on external walls, ideally a boiling-water-resistant or boiling-water-proof plywood grade.
- **Sealed edges**, including those inside sink cabinets and at the back of wardrobes.
- **Laminate or acrylic shutters**, which cope with damp and frequent cleaning.
- **Corrosion-resistant hardware** from a known brand.
- **Ventilation**: air gaps behind wardrobes, louvred shoe racks and exhaust fans in wet areas.

Schedule painting and polishing for the months after the rains where possible.

## Budgets and quotes in Panvel

Panvel is in Raigad district. The Economic Survey of Maharashtra puts Raigad's per capita income at ₹3,15,681 in 2023-24, fifth among the state's district figures{{f:panvel-district-income}}. Budgets still vary widely between households, and the scope of a fit-out matters far more than any average.

**Get itemised quotes on the same plan.** Each unit should list its size, board grade and brand, shutter finish, hardware brand and countertop.

**Separate the extras.** Painting, false ceilings, electrical changes and civil work should be separate lines.

**Pay in stages.** Booking, signed drawings, approved samples, delivery, installation and a closed snag list are natural milestones.

**Fix the schedule around your move.** If you are paying rent elsewhere until the flat is ready, ask for a dated schedule and what happens if a stage slips.

**Get the warranty in writing**, product by product.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Panvel Municipal Corporation include Kharghar and Kamothe?

Yes. The corporation includes 29 revenue villages and the CIDCO colonies of Taloja, Kharghar, Kalamboli, Kamothe and New Panvel{{f:panvel-pmc}}.

### What should I check when I take possession of a new flat?

Electrical points and switches, seepage around windows and bathrooms, hollow or uneven tiles, and the builder's electrical and plumbing drawings. Get defects fixed before the designer starts.

### Will the airport affect my flat?

It depends on your location and the flight paths. If noise is a concern, ask about acoustic glazing and use soft furnishings. The airport began commercial flights on 25 December 2025{{f:panvel-airport}}.

### Which materials suit Panvel's monsoon?

Moisture-resistant plywood with sealed edges, laminate or acrylic shutters and corrosion-resistant hardware. At the nearest IMD observatory, about 96% of the year's rain falls from June to September{{f:panvel-imd-monsoon}}.

### Should I move the builder's electrical points?

Move any that do not suit your furniture layout, before painting and before wardrobes are built. It is much cheaper at this stage.
