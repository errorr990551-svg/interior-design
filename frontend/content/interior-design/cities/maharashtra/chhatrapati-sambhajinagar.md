> **The short version.** Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, still widely called Aurangabad, has more renters than most Maharashtra cities: 33.5% of households rented in the 2011 Census{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-rented}}. Its IMD observatory at Chikalthana records a dry April, with evening humidity averaging 27%{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-humidity}}, and a monsoon that peaks in July{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-monsoon}}. Decide first whether you are designing for yourself or for a tenant, because the right materials and spend differ.

## Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar's homes and households

Aurangabad Municipal Corporation, the city's name in the 2011 Census, had 1,175,116 residents{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-census-pop}} in 226,073 households{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-total}}. The Census counts living rooms, bedrooms, dining, drawing and study rooms as dwelling rooms, and leaves out kitchens, bathrooms, store rooms and verandahs{{f:census-room-definition}}.

| Dwelling rooms per household | Share of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar households |
|---|---|
| One room, or no exclusive room{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-small}} | 39.7% |
| Two rooms{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-two}} | 27.8% |
| Three or more rooms{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-large}} | 32.5% |

About two in five households had a single room or none of their own{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-small}}, while nearly a third had three rooms or more{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-large}}. Ownership stood at 64.2%{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-owned}}, with a third of households renting{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-rented}}.

That mix produces two quite different clients. One is an owner-occupier planning furniture to last a decade or more. The other is a landlord, or a tenant on a lease, who needs a home that works now and survives a change of occupant without expensive repairs. A good brief starts by saying which one you are.

## Furnishing a flat you plan to let

If the home will be rented out, durability and easy upkeep matter more than statement finishes.

**Fit the fixed things well, keep the rest loose.** A modular kitchen, bedroom wardrobes and a shoe cabinet add value for any tenant. Sofas, beds and dining sets can be left to the occupant, or bought as loose furniture that is easy to replace.

**Choose finishes that hide wear.** Mid-tone laminates with a light texture show fewer scratches and fingerprints than high-gloss acrylic or pale veneer. A sage or oak-look shutter, for example, looks fresh for longer than white gloss.

{{img:wardrobe}}

**Keep walls neutral and repairable.** Washable emulsion in a standard shade can be touched up between tenants without repainting the whole flat. Note the shade code and keep it with the lease papers.

**Use standard sizes and branded hardware.** Hinges, channels and handles from a known brand can be replaced quickly when one fails, instead of rebuilding a shutter.

**Skip heavy wall panelling.** Fluted panels and wallpaper rarely survive several moves of furniture. Put the money into storage instead.

## A dry spring and a July monsoon

The India Meteorological Department's 1991–2020 normals for the Chikalthana observatory show 811.7 mm of rain a year over about 46 rainy days{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-rain}}.

| Season | What the IMD data shows | What it means for interiors |
|---|---|---|
| Monsoon{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-monsoon,chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-humidity}} | 677.5 mm from June to September, about 83% of the year's rain; July is the wettest month at 178.0 mm; morning humidity of 85% in July and 87% in August | Boards near sinks and outer walls take up moisture; closed cupboards turn musty |
| Hot season{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-heat,chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-humidity}} | May averages a daily high of 39.6 °C; April evenings average 27% humidity | Solid wood shrinks, joints open slightly, fabrics fade near west windows |
| Winter{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-winter}} | January nights average 12.4 °C | Cool, dry weather that suits painting, polishing and wallpaper |

The swing from dry April evenings to damp August mornings{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-humidity}} is what loosens edge banding and makes doors stick in some seasons and gap in others.

**Seal every edge.** Ask for edge banding on backs and undersides as well as the visible faces, so panels move evenly as the air changes.

**Ventilate shoe racks and utility cupboards.** Louvred or perforated shutters keep damp air from settling during the monsoon.

**Plan site finishes for winter.** Cool January weather{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-winter}} gives paint and polish a steady drying spell.

## Materials for owners who plan to stay

For owner-occupiers, who made up nearly two-thirds of households{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-owned}}, the extra cost of better materials is spread over many years of use.

- **Moisture-resistant plywood where water reaches.** A boiling-water-resistant or boiling-water-proof grade for the kitchen sink base, bathroom vanities and wardrobes on outer walls.
- **Engineered boards for large panels.** They move far less than solid wood between dry and humid months.
- **Quartz or granite countertops.** Both handle heat and daily cleaning better than laminate tops.
- **Soft-close hardware from a known brand.** It costs more at the start but lasts through years of daily use.
- **Termite-treated boards and a pre-fitting treatment.** Especially for ground-floor homes and independent houses.
- **Lighting planned in layers.** Ceiling lights for cleaning, task lights over counters and desks, and softer lamps for the evening.

## Budgets and quotes in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Aurangabad district, now Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar district, had a per capita income of ₹2,35,677 in 2023-24, 13th of the 34 district figures in Maharashtra's Economic Survey{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-district-income}}. A district average tells you little about one flat, so compare quotes on the details instead.

**Set the purpose before the budget.** A rental and a family home justify different spending on the same floor plan.

**Get every quote on the same drawings.** Each line should name the unit size, board grade and brand, shutter finish, hardware brand and countertop.

**Keep civil work separate.** Painting, electrical changes and any waterproofing should be priced as their own lines.

**Link payments to milestones.** Booking, approved drawings, samples, delivery, installation and the closed snag list are sensible stages.

**For rentals, ask for a handover file.** Laminate codes, paint shades, hardware brands and the written warranty make later repairs quick.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I furnish a flat before renting it out?

Fit the kitchen and wardrobes, which tenants value, and leave most loose furniture out. With a third of the city's households renting in 2011{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-hh-rented}}, a well-fitted but simple flat suits many tenants.

### Which shutter finish lasts best in a rental?

A mid-tone textured laminate. It hides scratches and fingerprints better than high-gloss acrylic or pale veneer and is cheap to replace if one shutter is damaged.

### Why do my wardrobe doors stick in August?

The boards take up moisture. Morning humidity averages 87% in August at the Chikalthana observatory{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-humidity}}. Sealed edges, moisture-resistant plywood and a small gap behind outer-wall wardrobes help.

### When should painting be done?

The winter months, when the air is cool and dry. January nights average 12.4 °C at Chikalthana{{f:chhatrapati-sambhajinagar-imd-winter}}, and paint cures evenly in that weather.

### Is Aurangabad the same city as Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar?

Yes. The city was renamed, and the last Census and many older documents still use Aurangabad, which is why both names appear on this page.
