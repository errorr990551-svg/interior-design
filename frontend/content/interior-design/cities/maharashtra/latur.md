## Latur's homes and households

Latur Municipal Council had 382,940 residents in the 2011 Census{{f:latur-census-pop}}, in 71,871 households{{f:latur-hh-total}}. The Census counts living rooms, bedrooms, dining, drawing and study rooms as dwelling rooms, but not kitchens, bathrooms, store rooms or verandahs{{f:census-room-definition}}.

| Dwelling rooms per household | Share of Latur households |
|---|---|

| Two rooms{{f:latur-hh-two}} | 27.7% |

Roughly two in five households had one room or none of their own{{f:latur-hh-small}}, and a third had three rooms or more{{f:latur-hh-large}}. Most owned their home, at 67.1%{{f:latur-hh-owned}}, while 31.0% rented{{f:latur-hh-rented}}.

With so many renters alongside owners, budgets and timelines vary widely. A tenant may want only loose furniture that can move with them; an owner may be fitting out a whole family house. For both, a clear plan for what is built in and what stays movable avoids spending money in the wrong place.

## Rain that peaks in September

Latur has no IMD observatory with full 1991–2020 normals of its own{{f:latur-imd-rain}}. The nearest is at Udgir, also in Latur district, which records 954.1 mm of rain a year over about 53 rainy days{{f:latur-imd-rain}}. Conditions in the city may differ somewhat.

| Season | What Udgir's IMD normals show | What it means for interiors |
|---|---|---|
| Monsoon{{f:latur-imd-monsoon,latur-imd-humidity}} | 763.1 mm from June to September, about 80% of the year's rain; September is the wettest month at 227.5 mm; morning humidity of 83% in July and 85% in August | Damp walls late in the season; boards near water swell |
| Hot season{{f:latur-imd-heat,latur-imd-humidity}} | May averages a daily high of 39.6 °C; April evenings average 25% humidity | Wood shrinks, joints open, dust settles fast |
| Winter{{f:latur-imd-winter}} | December nights average 13.7 °C | Dry, steady weather for painting and polishing |

Because the heaviest rain comes in September{{f:latur-imd-monsoon}}, walls may stay damp well into October. Paint applied too soon can bubble or peel.

**Check walls before painting.** Ask your contractor to test damp-looking patches, especially on outer walls, before putty and paint go on after the rains.

**Finish waterproofing early.** Terrace, bathroom and external wall repairs are best done in the dry months before June.

**Move site finishes to winter.** Settled December weather{{f:latur-imd-winter}} suits polishing, painting and wallpaper.

## Built-in or movable: choosing what to fix

With nearly a third of households renting in 2011{{f:latur-hh-rented}}, the line between fixed and loose furniture matters more than in many cities.

**For tenants, keep it portable.** Freestanding wardrobes, a bookcase and a sofa-cum-bed can move to the next home. Gallery walls of framed prints decorate a room without drilling large holes or building panels.

**For owners, build in the essentials.** A modular kitchen, full-height wardrobes and a TV unit fixed to the wall use every wall efficiently and last for years.

**Choose easy-clean wall finishes.** A stone-look or laminate panel behind the TV or dining table wipes clean and protects the paint from scuffs.

**Plan sockets with the furniture.** Decide where the TV, router, desk lamp and phone chargers go before electrical work, so wires are not left trailing across walls.

**Keep a central store in larger homes.** A store room for suitcases, festival items and spare bedding avoids building lofts above every bedroom.

## Materials for dry springs and a damp autumn

Udgir's normals show both very dry spring evenings and humid monsoon mornings{{f:latur-imd-humidity}}, so materials need to cope with movement. Because the rains run late, boards also stay exposed to damp for longer than in cities where the monsoon tails off in August.

- **Engineered boards for large panels.** They move far less than solid wood when humidity swings.
- **Moisture-resistant plywood in wet areas.** A boiling-water-resistant or boiling-water-proof grade under sinks, in bathrooms and for wardrobes on outer walls.
- **Edge banding on every side.** Backs and undersides as well as the visible faces.
- **Laminate shutters.** They resist dust, heat and occasional damp better than untreated veneer.
- **Termite-treated boards.** Ask for factory treatment and an anti-termite treatment before fitting, especially on the ground floor.

## Budgets and quotes in Latur

Latur district had a per capita income of ₹1,93,152 in 2023-24, 19th of the 34 district figures in Maharashtra's Economic Survey{{f:latur-district-income}}. That is a district average, so focus on how quotes compare for your own home.

**Decide what moves and what stays.** Price built-in units and loose furniture separately, so you can see what you would leave behind if you move.

**Use one set of drawings for every quote.** Each line should give the unit size, board grade and brand, shutter finish, hardware brand and countertop.

**Keep waterproofing and painting separate.** They depend on the weather and should be scheduled and priced on their own.

**Pay against milestones.** Booking, drawings, samples, delivery, installation and the closed snag list are sensible stages.

**Ask how the schedule handles September.** And get the warranty in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why use Udgir's weather data for Latur?

Latur has no IMD observatory with full 1991–2020 normals of its own{{f:latur-imd-rain}}. Udgir, in the same district, is the nearest with a complete record.

### When should painting start after the monsoon?

Once outer walls have dried out properly, which may be well into October. At Udgir, September is the wettest month{{f:latur-imd-monsoon}}.

### I rent my home. What furniture should I buy?

Freestanding pieces that move with you: a wardrobe, a sofa-cum-bed, a bookcase and framed prints rather than built-in panels. In 2011, 31.0% of Latur households rented{{f:latur-hh-rented}}.

### Why do joints open in my furniture in April?

The air is very dry. April evenings average 25% humidity at Udgir{{f:latur-imd-humidity}}, so wood shrinks. Engineered boards with sealed edges move less.

### Which season suits finishing work?

Winter, when the air is dry and settled. December nights average 13.7 °C at Udgir{{f:latur-imd-winter}}.

### Should waterproofing wait until after the rains?

No. Terrace and outer wall repairs work best in the dry months before the monsoon, so they are done before the wettest weeks of the year arrive. Leave painting and polishing for the winter, once walls have properly dried out.
