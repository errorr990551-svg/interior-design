> **The short version.** Ahilyanagar, formerly Ahmednagar, gets little rain: its IMD observatory averages 608.1 mm of rain a year over about 33 rainy days{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-rain}}. April evenings are very dry, at 24% humidity{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-humidity}}, and December nights average just 11.0 °C{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-winter}}. Plan for dryness and a real cool season, not only for the monsoon.

## Ahilyanagar's homes and households

Ahmadnagar Municipal Corporation, as the 2011 Census spelt it, had 350,859 residents{{f:ahilyanagar-census-pop}} in 74,422 households{{f:ahilyanagar-hh-total}}. A Census dwelling room means a living room, bedroom, dining, drawing or study room; kitchens, bathrooms, store rooms and verandahs are not included{{f:census-room-definition}}.

The three groups are close in size: just under two in five households had a single room or none{{f:ahilyanagar-hh-small}}, and close to a third had three or more{{f:ahilyanagar-hh-large}}. Owners made up 68.3% of households{{f:ahilyanagar-hh-owned}}, and 28.9% rented{{f:ahilyanagar-hh-rented}}.

In practice that means projects range from fitting out a single room for a young couple to furnishing a whole family flat or house. What links them is the climate: long dry spells, strong spring heat and winter nights cool enough that people notice cold floors and draughty windows.

## Low rainfall, a dry spring and cool nights

The India Meteorological Department's 1991–2020 normals for Ahmednagar's observatory show 608.1 mm of rain a year over about 33 rainy days{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-rain}}.

| Season | What the IMD data shows | What it means for interiors |
|---|---|---|
| Monsoon{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-monsoon,ahilyanagar-imd-humidity}} | 499.2 mm from June to September, about 82% of the year's rain; September is the wettest month at 153.5 mm; morning humidity of 80% in July and 82% in August | A short damp season; protect wet areas and outer walls |
| Hot season{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-heat,ahilyanagar-imd-humidity}} | April averages a daily high of 38.8 °C; April evenings average 24% humidity | Wood dries and splits, dust settles fast, fabrics fade |
| Winter{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-winter}} | December nights average 11.0 °C | Cold floors and draughts; good weather for site finishes |

Two details stand out. The hottest month is April rather than May{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-heat}}, so summer shading should be in place early. And the wettest month is September{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-monsoon}}, so work that needs dry walls should not assume the rains end in August.

**Treat dryness as the main risk.** Engineered boards with every edge sealed handle very dry air far better than solid wood or natural veneer.

**Shade before the heat arrives.** Fit blinds or thermal-lined curtains on west and south windows by March.

**Seal gaps around windows.** Weather strips on frames cut both dust in summer and cold draughts in winter.

## Warmth and comfort for the cool season

Few Maharashtra guides need to talk about winter comfort, but December nights averaging 11.0 °C{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-winter}} make it worth planning for.

**Add warmth underfoot.** Tile and stone floors stay pleasantly cool in April but feel cold on winter mornings. A washable rug beside the bed and in the living area is a cheap fix that can be rolled away in summer.

**Use warm textures in bedrooms.** Cane, wood-look laminates and fabric headboards make a room feel warmer than glossy, hard surfaces, without changing the temperature at all.

**Choose curtains for both seasons.** Lined curtains that block afternoon sun in April also hold warmth in on December nights.

**Make small walls work.** In one- and two-room homes, a compact dressing table with a mirror on a narrow wall, or behind a door, saves floor space.

## Materials for a dry, dusty climate

With so little rain on so few days{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-rain}}, Ahilyanagar's furniture faces dry air for most of the year. Choose materials for that first, then protect the few places water reaches, such as the kitchen sink base and bathroom vanities.

- **Engineered boards for wardrobes and TV units.** Good plywood or high-density board moves much less than solid wood in dry months.
- **Moisture-resistant grades near water.** A boiling-water-resistant or boiling-water-proof plywood under sinks and in bathrooms.
- **Laminate shutters with a matt finish.** They show dust less than gloss and wipe clean quickly.
- **Closed storage.** Shutters and glass fronts keep dust off books, crockery and clothes.
- **Fade-resistant fabrics.** Strong spring sun bleaches upholstery, so use sheers or blinds in front of good curtains.

## Budgets and quotes in Ahilyanagar

Ahmednagar district, now Ahilyanagar district, had a per capita income of ₹2,30,819 in 2023-24, 14th of the 34 district figures in Maharashtra's Economic Survey{{f:ahilyanagar-district-income}}. Use quotes, not averages, to judge value for your own home.

**Spend on daily-use items first.** A working kitchen, enough wardrobe space and good lighting matter more than decorative panels.

**Ask for one set of drawings, quoted line by line.** Each unit should list size, board grade and brand, shutter finish, hardware brand and countertop.

**List civil and plumbing work separately.** Painting, geyser points, electrical changes and any waterproofing belong on their own lines.

**Pay in stages.** Tie payments to drawings, samples, delivery, installation and the closed snag list.

**Get the warranty in writing**, and ask whether it covers cracking, warping or edge lifting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does solid wood crack in Ahilyanagar?

The air is very dry for much of the year. Evening humidity averages 24% in April at the city's IMD observatory{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-humidity}}, so solid wood loses moisture and shrinks. Engineered boards with sealed edges are steadier.

### Do I need to plan for winter in my interiors?

It helps. December nights average 11.0 °C{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-winter}}, so lined curtains, rugs on tile floors and well-sealed windows make homes more comfortable.

### When is the monsoon heaviest?

September is the wettest month at Ahmednagar's observatory{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-monsoon}}, so allow for rain into the autumn when scheduling painting or waterproofing.

### When should summer shading be fitted?

Before April, which is the hottest month, with an average daily high of 38.8 °C{{f:ahilyanagar-imd-heat}}.

### Is Ahmednagar the same city as Ahilyanagar?

Yes. The city was renamed, and the last Census and many records still use Ahmadnagar or Ahmednagar.

### Do tile floors make sense in a cool winter?

Yes, for most homes. Tiles stay cool and easy to clean through the hot, dusty months. A few washable rugs beside beds and in the living area take the chill off winter mornings and can be rolled away when the heat returns.
